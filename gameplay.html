Gameplay

Everything you need to play

One gesture, a handful of rules, and a board that pushes back. Each demo below plays by itself.

Drag to chain

Press a tile and drag across touching tiles of the same colour. Chain 3 or more, then lift your finger to clear them. Tiles touch up, down, left and right, never diagonally.

Wrong turn? Drag back over the tile you came from to undo a step, then go another way.

Longer chains, bigger scores

Every extra tile makes the whole chain worth more, so one long chain beats several short ones. Chain a long one and you earn a Rainbow tile, which joins any colour.

Start the next chain quickly to build a streak multiplier on top. Start it before the streak window closes and the multiplier steps up; let it close and you begin again. The window gets shorter as the level gets harder, which is what makes Expert so tense.

Stuck? Shuffle

In Infinite, when no chains are left, tap Shuffle to mix the colours. The little number on the button is how many you have left.

No shuffles left and no moves? That is game over, so use them wisely. Harder levels start with fewer.

Bricks and cracked bricks

A brick can never be chained. Clear a chain right next to it twice to break it.

A cracked brick hides a coloured tile. One clear next to it breaks it open and frees the tile inside.

Hazard bombs

Chain a bomb into a clear before its fuse runs out. Every move uses up one dot.

At zero it explodes and turns the tiles around it into bricks. Bombs land on random tiles, so spot them early and plan a chain.

Gold bombs

Chain a gold bomb before its timer bar runs out and every tile of that colour disappears. The bar gets shorter on harder levels.

Each wiped tile scores like one of the tiles in your chain. Bricks are safe, and if time runs out it simply turns back into a normal tile.

Modes and levels

Pick your fight

Blitz is a sprint against the clock where every chain adds time. Infinite lasts until you run out of moves. Daily is one shared board with one try a day.

LevelWhat it is
BeginnerThe classic game with friendly gold bombs
MediumBricks and two kinds of bomb
HardMore bombs and bricks, less time
ExpertShort fuses, short timers. No mercy

Every mode is played on the same 8×8 board, on phones and tablets alike.

Gameplay

Everything you need to play

One gesture, a handful of rules, and a board that pushes back. Each demo below plays by itself.

Drag to chain

Press a tile and drag across touching tiles of the same colour. Chain 3 or more, then lift your finger to clear them. Tiles touch up, down, left and right, never diagonally.

Wrong turn? Drag back over the tile you came from to undo a step, then go another way.

Longer chains, bigger scores

Every extra tile makes the whole chain worth more, so one long chain beats several short ones. Chain a long one and you earn a Rainbow tile, which joins any colour.

Start the next chain quickly to build a streak multiplier on top. Start it before the streak window closes and the multiplier steps up; let it close and you begin again. The window gets shorter as the level gets harder, which is what makes Hard so tense.

Surge: beat the board clock

A clock counts down to the next surge. When it hits zero every colour reshuffles, so plan for the new board, not the old one.

Tiles ringed in amber are marked: if you have not chained them away, they turn into bricks. A red mark becomes a hazard bomb. Chaining a marked tile defends it and scores a bonus. Each surge comes a little sooner than the last, and there is no shuffle button: if no chain is left, the run ends.

Bricks

A brick is a wall that can never be chained. Clear a chain right next to it and it cracks.

Clear next to the cracked brick once more and it breaks, leaving a gap. Two clears and it is gone.

Hazard bombs

Chain a bomb into a clear before its fuse runs out. Every move uses up one dot.

At zero it explodes and turns the tiles around it into bricks. Nothing about a bomb is luck: in Surge it is announced with a red mark first, and in the Daily it lands on the same move for everyone.

Gold bombs

Chain a gold bomb before its timer bar runs out and every tile of that colour disappears. You earn one by chaining five times in a row, and the bar is shorter on Hard.

Each wiped tile scores like one of the tiles in your chain. Bricks are safe, and if time runs out it simply turns back into a normal tile.

Modes and levels

Pick your fight

Surge is the main mode: beat the board clock. Blitz is a 60 second sprint where every chain adds time. Daily is one shared board, with the same hazards on the same moves for everyone, and one try a day.

LevelWhat it is
EasyA roomy clock and gentle surges
HardA short clock, more bricks and hazard bombs

Every mode is played on the same 8×8 board, on phones and tablets alike.

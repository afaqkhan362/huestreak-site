Live duels

Same board. Sixty seconds. Send them trouble.

Two players, one seeded board, a plain 60-second clock. The higher score wins. Play a long chain or keep a streak going and you earn a weapon to send to the other player's board.

See the weaponsRanked ladder

Live duels are rolling out in stages, with friends first. The solo modes are always there.

How it works

Three steps

Find a match

Challenge a friend with their code, or tap Battle and be matched with someone close to your level. If nobody is around you practise against a recording.

Race the clock

You both get exactly the same starting board. Chain as fast and as long as you can for 60 seconds. No heat, no time added.

Win, earn trophies

The higher score wins and earns trophies. A close finish, a comeback or a big chain earns a few more.

Weapons

Earned by play, sent in a tap

Every weapon is an attack on the other player: nothing helps you, nothing shields you. A play counts its chain length plus your streak, and the best weapon it reaches is yours.

Cracked bricksCracked bricks land on their board. One clear next to each breaks it.
BricksSolid bricks land on their board. It takes two clears beside each to break one.
BombA bomb with a short fuse lands on their board. They must chain it away before it goes off.
ShuffleEvery colour on their board is reshuffled, and the chain they were planning is gone.
FreezeTheir board is dead for two seconds. Time to run up the score.
  • You keep what you earn. Weapons wait in your hand until you decide to send them.
  • A short cooldown. After you send, only two different kinds can go out in the next few seconds, so you choose the moment.
  • A warning before it lands. The other player sees what is coming a heartbeat before it arrives, so it is a fight, not a trap.
Fair by design

A referee you can trust

The server runs the exact same rules as your phone for both players, move by move. Nobody can claim a chain or a weapon they did not earn, the clock is the server's, and both players' moves are saved and checked after every match.

  • Same board. Both players start from the same seeded board.
  • Same rules. One rules engine for the game, the phones and the server.
  • No chat. Quick emotes only, so duels stay friendly.

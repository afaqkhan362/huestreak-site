Your data

Delete my data

Huestreak has no sign-up, so your data is tied to a random player ID on your device. You can ask us to delete everything linked to it: your player record, display name, scores, game records and gameplay events.

How to ask

  1. Email support@huestreak.com with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Tell us your display name (if you set one) and, if you can, your player ID. Without an ID we may not be able to tell which records are yours, and we will tell you if so.

We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days. Leaderboard entries are removed with the account.

Delete it yourself, right now

Deleting the game from your device removes the local data and the secret key that links the device to your player ID. After that, nobody can use that player ID from your device. Remaining server records can be deleted on request as above.

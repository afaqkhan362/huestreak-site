Help with Huestreak

Something not working, a score that looks wrong, or a question? Email support@huestreak.com and a person will answer.

When you write, please include

  • Your device and operating system version
  • What you were doing and what happened
  • A screenshot, if you can

Common questions

Why is my score not on the leaderboard?

Scores are sent to our server and checked before they are ranked. If you were offline, the game keeps the score and sends it when you are next connected. The Daily Challenge needs a connection to start.

I played the Daily and cannot play again.

Each player has one attempt a day. A new board arrives at midnight UTC.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is tied to the game on your device.

Why was a game of mine rejected?

Every ranked game is replayed on our server. If the moves do not add up (for example a move too fast for a human, or a score that does not match the moves), it is not ranked. If you think we got it wrong, write to us.

See Delete my data.

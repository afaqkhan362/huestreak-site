Daily

One board. One attempt.

Everyone in the world plays the same board each day, with hazards that land on the same move for all of us. Make your single attempt count.

Try the demoHow to play

How a game goes

Same board for everyone

The day’s board is fixed, so scores can be compared fairly.

Read the warnings

A hazard bomb is announced ("lands in 3 moves") before it arrives, so nothing is luck.

One attempt

When the board locks up your score goes on the leaderboard. Come back tomorrow for a new board.

On the board

  • Leaderboard. See where you stand against everyone, and against friends.
  • Champion crown. Win a busy Daily and the crown sits beside your name until the next day is settled.
  • Verified. Your game is replayed by the server, so every score on the board is real.

Blitz

A sprint that heats up.

A short clock starts with your first chain. Every chain pays time back, but the longer you play, the hotter it gets.

Try the demoHow to play

How a game goes

Start the clock

It starts with your first chain, so take a moment to read the board.

Refill it

Each chain adds time back. Long, fast chains add the most.

Feel the heat

Heat builds with the seconds you have played and with long fast chains. The clock runs faster, chains pay less and extra colours join the board.

On the board

  • Combos. Back-to-back chains build a multiplier and show the time you gained.
  • Hazard bombs. They appear as the heat rises: chain them into a clear.
  • Short runs. A good run is under a minute, which makes it the natural "one more go".

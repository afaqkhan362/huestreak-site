Three ways to play

A quick break, a sprint, a daily puzzle

Every mode is the same simple gesture with a different kind of pressure.

Main mode

Surge

A board clock counts down. When it hits zero every colour reshuffles, and the tiles you did not save turn to bricks.

 Sprint

Blitz

A short clock that you refill with every chain. The longer you last, the hotter it gets.

 Once a day

Daily

One board for everyone, one attempt, hazards that land on the same move for all. See where you rank.
Live duels

Same board. Sixty seconds. Send them trouble.

Race a friend, or anyone online, on the exact same board. Long chains and streaks earn weapons you can send straight to the other player's board.

  • Fair by design. Both players start on the same board and a server referee checks every move.
  • Earn weapons. Cracked bricks, bricks, bombs, shuffles and freezes.
  • Earn trophies. Win, and play well, to climb.
How duels work
Ranked

Climb from Spark to Spectrum

Seasons last four weeks. Play your placement matches, find your tier and chase the next step. Ranked is earned: win live duels to earn trophies and unlock it.

Fair and calm

Built so you can trust the score

Every score is checked

Each game is replayed by the server with the same rules, so a leaderboard score is a score somebody really earned.

No ads. Ever.

No pop-ups between games, no timers that nag you to come back, no tracking cookies on this site.

Readable on purpose

Every tile can be told apart at a glance, with optional symbols on each colour, and a reduce-motion setting for calmer clears.

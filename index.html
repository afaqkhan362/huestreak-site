How it plays

Easy to start. Hard to put down.

One gesture does everything. The depth is in what you chain, and how fast you chain the next one.

Drag to chain

Press a tile and drag across touching tiles of the same colour. Chain 3 or more, then lift your finger to clear them. Wrong turn? Drag back to undo a step.

Build a streak

Longer chains are worth more for every tile. Start the next chain inside the streak window and your multiplier keeps climbing.

Survive the board

Bricks lock tiles in place. Hazard bombs turn their neighbours to bricks. Gold bombs clear a whole colour, if you are quick. Shuffles are scarce.

Modes

Three ways to play

Blitz

Race the clock: every chain you clear adds seconds back.

Infinite

No timer, no pressure. Play until the board runs out of moves, and use your shuffles wisely.

Daily

Everyone gets the same board. You get one attempt a day, and the whole world is on the same leaderboard.

Levels

Pick how hard it fights back

Every mode has four levels. Harder levels bring more hazards and give you less time to keep a streak alive.

Beginner

The classic game with friendly gold bombs.

Medium

Bricks and two kinds of bomb.

Hard

More bombs and bricks, less time.

Expert

Short fuses, short timers. No mercy.

Leaderboards you can trust

Every ranked game is issued by our server and replayed on our server before the score counts. Edited scores and impossible moves are thrown out, so a rank means something.

A world to climb

Global boards for every mode and level, plus the Daily: the same board for everyone, reset at midnight UTC. No account needed to start playing.

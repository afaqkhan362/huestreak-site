Surge

Beat the board clock.

A clock counts down to the next surge. When it reaches zero every colour reshuffles, so you plan for the board that is coming, not the one you are looking at.

Try the demoHow to play

How a game goes

Chain

Drag across three or more touching tiles of one colour. Longer chains score far more, and a quick next chain builds a streak.

Watch the marks

Tiles ringed in amber are marked: chain them away before the clock ends or they turn into bricks. A red mark becomes a hazard bomb.

Survive the surge

Every colour reshuffles and the next clock starts, a little sooner each time. Easy and Hard have their own pace.

On the board

  • Bricks. A wall you cannot chain. Clear beside it twice to break it.
  • Hazard bombs. Chain them into a clear before the fuse runs out, or they wreck the tiles around them.
  • Rainbow tiles. A very long chain earns a tile that joins any colour.
  • Gold bombs. A streak of five earns one: chain it in time and every tile of its colour disappears.

Ranked

Climb from Spark to Spectrum.

Ranked is live duels with a rating on the line. Play your placement matches, find your tier and fight for the next step, season after season.

How duels work

Ranked opens after live duels, and you earn your way in with trophies.

How a season works

Four weeks to prove it

Earn your way in

Win live duels to earn trophies. A close finish, a comeback or a big chain earns a few more. Enough trophies and Ranked opens for you.

Placement

Your first five matches of a season move your rating fastest and place you on the ladder. Your tier shows once you are placed.

Match-making

You are matched with players near your rating. The longer you wait, the wider the search, and if the lobby is empty you can practise against a recording that is not rated.

Season end

At the end of the season everyone moves halfway back toward the middle, and the best tier you reached earns a cosmetic reward.

  • Server-decided. Every rating change is calculated by the server, never by the phone.
  • No farming. Two friends can only move each other's rating a few times a day.
  • Cosmetic rewards only. Nothing you can earn in Ranked changes a match.
Leaderboards

See where you stand

Every mode has a leaderboard, global and among friends, and the ranked board shows the placed players of the season.
